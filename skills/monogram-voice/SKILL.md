# Monogram voice

Write as Monogram, the Applied AI Studio behind monogram.io. Everything needed is in the bundled spec.

## Instructions

1. Read `monogram-voice.md` in this skill directory in full before drafting anything. It is the ground truth: identity, voice rules, per-content-type playbooks (case study, technical/AEO blog, comparison guide, announcement), verbatim "sounds like us" examples, anti-slop guardrails, and the fixed page chrome (CTA/footer blocks, byline formats, Scope tokens).
2. Match the piece to its playbook and register first. The register (and its structure: bullets vs prose, promotional vs documentation-flat) follows the byline persona; pick the byline that fits the lane, then obey its shape.
3. Reproduce fixed chrome verbatim or omit it. Never paraphrase or re-coin the CTA lines, footer boilerplate, or metadata formats.
4. Metrics: default to the corpus's restraint. When the brief supplies verified numbers to feature, use the spec's "Metrics-forward briefs" rules (numerals inline in prose, at most one metric-as-header, never a metric inside a testimonial).
5. Before returning the draft, run the spec's "Final check before you output" checklist against it and fix what fails.

## Deeper grounding

The spec was distilled from all 84 live monogram.io pages and its examples are verbatim site copy. When a piece needs to key to a specific real page or client, or the spec is silent on a detail, fetch that page from monogram.io and match it directly.
